Bedrock is a bedtime app blocker for people who are done losing their nights to their phone.

Set the hours you want to be off your phone. During that window, Bedrock blocks the apps that keep you scrolling - social media, video, games, whatever you choose - so putting the phone down at night becomes the easy choice instead of the hard one.

It does one thing, and it does it quietly.

No tracking, ever.
Bedrock collects nothing about you. No account, no analytics, no "insights," no charts of your screen time. It never looks at what you do on your phone, and nothing ever leaves your device. Most screen time apps are built on watching you. Bedrock is built on leaving you alone.

Radically minimal.
The app is calm by design - plain, monotone, nothing competing for your attention. It is the opposite of the apps it blocks. A tool meant to protect your sleep shouldn't be another bright, noisy thing on your phone.

Built for people who are serious.
This isn't a gentle "screen time awareness" nudge. Bedrock is uncompromising by design. When your blocked hours start, the apps are locked, with no easy way out and no exceptions to talk yourself into at midnight. It's for people genuinely committed to better sleep and ready to take a hard line with their phone at night.

Never truly locked out.
If a real emergency comes up, you can unblock for one dollar. The point was never the money - it's a small, deliberate bit of friction, just enough to break the reflex to reach for your phone, while never leaving you stuck when you genuinely need in.

What Bedrock does:
- Blocks the apps you choose during the hours you set
- Runs automatically every night, on your schedule
- Keeps everything on your device - no data collected, no ads, no account
- Uncompromising by default - once your blocked hours start, the apps stay locked
- One-dollar emergency unblock for the moments that truly need it

Bedrock is a social media blocker for people who are done losing hours to the feed.

The problem was never the apps. It is the feed - the endless, algorithmic scroll that Reels, Shorts and every home timeline are built around. You open the app to message a friend and leave forty minutes later having spoken to no one.

So Bedrock blocks the feed, not the friendship.

Block the scroll, keep the people.
Turn on feed blocking and Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and the home feed stop opening. Your messages, your search, your profile all still work. You can still talk to the people you actually opened the app for. This is the part other app blockers cannot do - they are all or nothing, so you either lose your friends or keep the scroll.

Block whole apps too, on your schedule.
Set the hours you want to be off your phone. During that downtime window, Bedrock blocks the apps you choose - social media, video, games, whatever keeps you scrolling. Anything you mark Always Allowed stays open the whole time.

No tracking, ever.
Bedrock collects nothing about you. No account, no analytics, no upload. It never measures your screen time and never records what you open. Nothing leaves your device, because there is nowhere for it to go. Most screen time apps are built on watching you. Bedrock is built on leaving you alone.

Radically minimal.
The app is calm by design - plain, monotone, nothing competing for your attention. It is the opposite of the apps it blocks. A tool meant to get you off the feed should not be another bright, noisy thing on your phone.

Never truly locked out.
Getting back in during a block is deliberately slow, but never impossible. There is a passcode you set, a free unblock that takes some patience to reach, and a one dollar emergency unblock for when you genuinely need in right now. The point was never the money. It is friction, just enough to break the reflex, and a free way out always exists.

What Bedrock does:
- Blocks Reels, Shorts and home feeds while leaving messaging usable
- Blocks whole apps during the downtime hours you set
- Runs automatically on your schedule, every day
- Keeps everything on your device - no data collected, no ads, no account
- Friction on the way back in, with a free unblock that is always available

Close the feed. Keep the phone.

Bedrock - a social media blocker that takes the scroll, not your friends.

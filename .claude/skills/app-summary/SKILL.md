# What Bedrock does

Bedrock is a bedtime app blocker for Android. You set your own bed and wake times for each day of the week, and during that window Bedrock locks away the apps that keep you up - social media, games, whatever you choose - so your phone stops fighting your sleep. It runs quietly in the background, never collects data about you, and always leaves a safety valve (a $1 emergency bypass) so you're never truly locked out when you genuinely need your phone.

Privacy Policy for Bedrock

Last updated: 28 July 2026

Bedrock is a social media and app blocker published by Grounded Labs. This policy explains what Bedrock does and does not do with your information. The short version: Bedrock collects nothing about you.

Information we collect

None. Bedrock does not collect, store, transmit, or sell any personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers, and no third-party SDKs that profile you. We do not know who you are, and we cannot see how you use your phone.

Information stored on your device

Your settings - such as your blocking schedule and which apps you have chosen to allow - are saved only on your own device. This information never leaves your phone and is never uploaded to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the app removes it.

Permissions the app uses

To block apps during the times you set, Bedrock needs certain Android permissions, including an Accessibility Service. Bedrock uses these solely to detect when a blocked app is opened and to show its own blocking screen instead. Bedrock does not read, record, or collect the content of your screen, your keystrokes, your messages, or anything you do inside other apps. All processing happens on your device in real time; nothing is logged or sent anywhere.

Payments

Bedrock offers a one-dollar emergency unblock as an in-app purchase. All payments are processed entirely by Google Play Billing. Bedrock never sees, handles, or stores your payment details. Any information you provide during a purchase is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy.

Children

Bedrock is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
